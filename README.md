# Electromagnetic Characterization of a Radially-Slotted Circular Microstrip Patch Antenna in the C-Band

## Project overview
This project studies the electromagnetic behavior of a circular microstrip patch antenna with eight tilted rectangular radial slots. The paper investigates multiband operation across the 5–7 GHz C-band range using ANSYS HFSS simulations.

The ground plane uses a similarly patterned geometry rotated by 7 degrees, and the antenna is excited using a coaxial probe feed.

## Design details reported in the paper
- **Antenna type:** Radially-slotted circular microstrip patch antenna
- **Simulation software:** Ansys HFSS
- **Frequency sweep:** 5–7 GHz
- **Substrate:** Rogers RT/duroid 5880
- **Substrate thickness:** 1.57 mm
- **Relative permittivity:** 2.2
- **Number of radial slots:** 8
- **Slot tilt angle:** 75°
- **Ground-plane rotation:** 7°
- **Feed:** Coaxial probe feed

The paper reports a circular section radius of 149 mm and rectangular slots of length 92 mm and breadth approximately 25 mm. Verify these dimensions against the original HFSS design before using them to reproduce the antenna, as the values should be checked against the intended geometry and operating frequency.

## Reported simulation results
- **Dominant resonance:** approximately 5.80 GHz
- **Return loss at dominant resonance:** approximately −25.68 dB
- **Peak realized gain at 5.80 GHz:** approximately 8.8 dBi
- **Reported usable impedance bandwidth:** approximately 70 MHz (5.77–5.84 GHz)
- **Polarization:** Linear, according to the axial-ratio discussion
- **Other resonances:** the paper describes resonant bands near 5.55 GHz and 6.40 GHz

The paper's gain discussion also refers to gain patterns at 5.52 GHz, 5.80 GHz, and 6.32 GHz. These values differ slightly from the abstract and conclusion, so retain the values from the relevant figure or simulation when presenting results.

## Analysis included
- S11 / return-loss response
- Surface-current distribution
- Three-dimensional gain patterns
- Axial-ratio response
- Parametric sweeps for slot angle and slot width

## Limitations noted in the paper
- The impedance bandwidth is relatively narrow (about 70 MHz).
- Multiple elevation-plane lobes appear in the radiation patterns.
- The antenna is linearly polarized and does not demonstrate circular polarization in the reported operating band.
